# Trader Behavior Insights Summary

## Methodology
- Cleaned and standardized sentiment + historical trade data.
- Aligned by date and engineered daily trader behavior/performance metrics.
- Compared Fear vs Greed outcomes and segmented traders.

## Key Insights
- Fear mean daily pnl: 5185.15
- Greed mean daily pnl: 4144.21
- Fear mean win rate: 0.8423
- Greed mean win rate: 0.8563

## Strategy Recommendations
1. During Fear days, keep normal sizing for profitable segments; tighten risk on Greed days.
2. Allocate more risk budget to ConsistentWinner profile and cap position size for Inconsistent profile.
3. For low win-rate high-frequency traders, enforce max trades/day and smaller position size until recovery.
